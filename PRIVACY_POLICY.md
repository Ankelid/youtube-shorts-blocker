# Privacy Policy for YouTube Shorts Blocker

Last updated: 2026

**YouTube Shorts Blocker** is an open-source browser extension designed to hide YouTube Shorts interface elements and prevent accidental Shorts playback.

### Data Collection & Usage
- **No Personal Data Collected:** The extension does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information, browsing history, keystrokes, or YouTube account details.
- **Local Storage:** The extension only uses Chrome's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`) solely on your device to remember your interface preferences (e.g., active toggles and selected language). This data never leaves your computer.
- **No Third-Party Analytics:** We do not embed any analytics, tracking pixels, ads, or third-party tracking scripts.

### Contact
If you have any questions, feel free to open an issue in this GitHub repository.
